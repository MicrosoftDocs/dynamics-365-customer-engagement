# Get started with Customer Service Community

The Customer Service Community app helps your organization invite customer suggestions and crowdsource ideas to shape the future of the products they use.

> [!NOTE]
> To install Customer Service Community, you must have a Dynamics 365 organization and ensure that the organization tenant has at least one Customer Service Enterprise license. For information about licensing, refer to the [Dynamics 365 Licensing Guide](https://go.microsoft.com/fwlink/?LinkId=866544).

## Install Customer Service Community

Complete the following steps:

1. Go to the [Microsoft Marketplace Dynamics 365 Customer Service Community download page](https://go.microsoft.com/fwlink/?linkid=2168784).
1. Select **Get it now**, and then sign in with your Dynamics System Admin account.
   
   :::image type="content" source="../media/community-get-started.png" alt-text="Screenshot of Microsoft Marketplace page for Dynamics 365 Customer Service Community with the option to get and install the app.":::
   
1. Select the environment where you want to install Customer Service Community, accept the terms, and then select **Install**.

## Provision a Modern Community website

1.	Confirm that Customer Service Community is successfully installed in the previous procedure.
1.	Go to [Power Pages](https://make.powerpages.microsoft.com/), and then select the environment where you installed Customer Service Community.
1.	Go to the **Templates** tab, and then select the **Dynamics 365** tab.
1.	Select **Modern Community** > **Choose this template**.
1.	Select **Done**. Site provisioning typically takes a few minutes. When it completes, it displays in the **Active Sites** list under **Home**.

When your Modern Community website is created, you can view the Community home page by going to https://&lt;name&gt;.powerappsportals.com/d365community.
