# AI agents in Dynamics 365 Field Service

AI agents are intelligent tools that understand natural language, automate tasks, and provide quick insights. They reduce manual effort by interpreting user intent, making decisions, and optimizing workflows.

[!INCLUDE [ai-agents-dynamics-365-field-service](../../ce-shared/ai-agents-dynamics-365-field-service.md)]

## Field Service capabilities in Service Agent (preview)

Service Agent supports conversational Field Service workflows for technicians, dispatchers, and service managers. Early scenarios include reviewing the day's bookings, understanding work orders and work history, capturing reviewable updates, and coordinating supported service actions.

These capabilities are part of Service Agent, not a separate Field Service bot or the Scheduling Operations Agent. They support dedicated technician workflows as well as Customer Service-to-Field Service scenarios.

Learn more in [Field Service capabilities in Service Agent](service-agent-overview.md) and [Request public preview access](service-agent-preview.md).
