# View costs associated with a work order

Use the Cost Summary on a work order to compare what you expected the work to cost with what you actually incur as the work progresses. The Cost Summary brings together estimated and actual costs across labor, services, products and materials, time, and approved expenses. Service managers get a more complete view of the financial impact of the work directly from the work order.

You can review the Cost Summary throughout the work order lifecycle as estimates change and actual transactions are recorded.

The Cost Summary helps you:

- Compare planned and actual costs throughout execution.
- Identify cost variance before the work is completed.
- Understand which parts of the work contribute to overall cost.
- Reduce the need to reconcile service activity and project cost information across separate experiences.
- Make more informed decisions while the work is still underway.

## Access a cost summary for a work order

1. In Field Service, open the work order.

1. Select the **Cost Summary** tab.

1. Select **Refresh Totals** in the command bar to update the cost summary with the latest data.

   :::image type="content" source="media/project-operations-integration-work-order-cost-summary.png" alt-text="Screenshot of the Cost Summary screen with the Refresh Totals button highlighted." lightbox="media/project-operations-integration-work-order-cost-summary.png":::

**Effort (Hours)** under **Estimated** reflects the planned effort entered on the project task. **Effort (Completed Hours)** under **Actual** reflects approved time entries recorded against the work.
