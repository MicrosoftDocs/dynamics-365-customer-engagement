# Dynamics 365 Field Service in US government clouds

Microsoft strives to maintain functional parity between commercially available services and those enabled through the United States government clouds, specifically:

- US Government Community Cloud (GCC)

- US GCC High (GCCH)

- US Department of Defense (US DoD)

Exceptions exist to the principle of maintaining product functional parity within the U.S. government clouds. Microsoft continues to evaluate these services and capabilities for inclusion and updates in future releases. The following table lists which Field Service services are currently supported in each cloud.

| Feature | GCC | GCCH | DoD |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dynamics 365 Field Service | Available | Available | Available |
| Connected Field Service | Available | Available | Available |
| Field Service Mobile Application – Android | Available | Available | Available |
| Field Service Mobile Application – iOS | Available | Available | Available |
| Field Service Mobile Application – Windows | Available | Available | Available |
| Resource Scheduling Optimization | Not Available | Not Available | Not Available |
| Universal Resource Scheduling | Available | Available | Available |
| Scheduling Operations Agent | Not Available | Not Available | Not Available |
| Field Service Rentals | Not Available | Not Available | Not Available |
| Field Service and Project Operations Integration | Not Available | Not Available | Not Available |
| Field Service Portal | Not Available | Not Available | Not Available |
| Field Service Custom Controls | Not Available | Not Available | Not Available |
| Universal Scheduling Service | Not Available | Not Available | Not Available |
