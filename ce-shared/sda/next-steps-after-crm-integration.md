After the MCP server is published to your environment, the Sales Development agent can now interact with Dynamics 365. You can proceed to [onboard the Sales Development agent](../../ce/sales/sales-dev-agent/onboard-agent.md) directly in Teams to configure its playbook, guidelines, product knowledge, and settings.

> [!NOTE]
> - Chat with the agent in Teams and tell it to onboard to the CRM. For example, you can say "Onboard to CRM." or "Connect to CRM."
> - Ensure to set the default product recommendation for the agent.
